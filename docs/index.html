About Us

01 — Who we are

The O Commons: Immigrant Artists Mutual Aid is a community-driven initiative that responds to the growing precarity of working as an artist or scholar on an O‑1 visa, one of the main U.S. work visas for individuals with extraordinary ability in the arts. In today's gig-based cultural economy, many artists rely on multiple jobs, freelance work, and institutional affiliations to make a living. O‑1 visa holders, however, are often restricted to a narrow range of authorized employment. As fewer cultural institutions are willing or able to sponsor O‑1 visa holders and as the cost and complexity of maintaining legal status continue to rise, immigrant artists increasingly find themselves excluded from employment opportunities, forced to navigate an opaque immigration system, and left without access to the practical knowledge and a support system that is needed to sustain their careers.

Immigration attorneys provide essential legal expertise. However, much of the legal process depends on practical knowledge that is rarely shared publicly: timelines, recommendation letters, supportive institutions, and the experiences of artists and scholars who successfully obtained their O visas.

The O Commons fills this gap with peer-to-peer mutual aid and shared digital resources.

Submit Your Questions

05 — Ask anything

No question is too small. No situation too specific.

Ask about petitions, timelines, sponsors, attorneys, RFEs, or daily life on an O‑1. Anonymized questions and answers become part of the public Resource Hub — so the next artist starts further ahead than you did.

Is this legal advice? +
No. Everything shared is experience-based peer knowledge, clearly distinguished from legal counsel. Mentors are oriented to keep it that way — it helps you ask better questions and prepare stronger consultations with attorneys.
Who can submit a question? +
Immigrant artists, cultural workers, international students, and allies — at any stage of the O‑1, EB, or student-to-visa path.
Will my question be public? +
Only if you choose. Selected questions are anonymized and added to the Resource Hub so answers can help the whole community.
