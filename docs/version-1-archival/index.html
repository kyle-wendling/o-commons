01 — Who we are

About Us

The O Commons is a mutual aid network for immigrant artists on O‑1 visas — built on the idea that practical knowledge, shared peer‑to‑peer, can make an opaque and isolating process transparent, collaborative, and equitable.

The problem

Narrow fields, rising costs

Fewer institutions sponsor O‑1 holders, and maintaining status is costly and complex. Employment is restricted to an exceptionally narrow field, which privileges artists with safety nets and excludes equally accomplished artists without them.

Who it affects

Three communities

Artists who built their lives in the U.S. and must renew again and again. Graduating international students who want to stay in the communities they built. Newly arriving artists who need authorization before their first engagement can begin.

What we do

Peer knowledge, shared

Attorneys provide legal expertise. We share everything around it: timelines, recommendation letters, supportive institutions, and the lived experience of artists who navigated the process before you.
