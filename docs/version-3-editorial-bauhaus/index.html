About Us

Working as an artist on an O‑1 visa is increasingly precarious. Fewer institutions can sponsor, legal status is costly to maintain, and employment is restricted to a narrow authorized field — a system that favors artists with economic safety nets and leaves equally accomplished artists behind.

Attorneys provide the legal expertise. But much of the process depends on practical knowledge that is rarely shared publicly: understanding timelines, preparing recommendation letters, identifying supportive institutions, and learning from those who navigated it before.

The O Commons builds an infrastructure of mutual aid through peer-to-peer knowledge sharing and a publicly accessible digital resource — making an opaque and isolating process transparent, collaborative, and equitable.

Immigration is not just a legal process. It is labor, emotion, money, and time — and it should not be carried alone.

03

Network

01

Peer-to-Peer Mutual Aid Network

Mentors who completed the O‑1 or employment-based EB process guide artists currently navigating it. Orientation keeps shared knowledge responsible, experience-based, and clearly distinguished from legal counsel — helping applicants ask informed questions and reduce legal costs.

 02

Resource Hub

A public archive of community-generated resources: attorneys recommended by artists, institutions that hire or sponsor O‑1 holders, anonymized petition materials, timelines, costs, RFE strategies, and a directory of artists willing to talk.

 03

Curatorial-Research Repository

Visa applications transformed into artworks, performances, and installations. A collective archive of document-based responses to migration — growing toward exhibitions, publications, and performances that expose the hidden labor behind artistic mobility.

Submit Your Questions

No question is too small. No situation too specific.

Ask about petitions, timelines, sponsors, attorneys, RFEs, or daily life on an O‑1. Selected questions — anonymized — become part of the public Resource Hub, so the next artist starts further ahead than you did.
